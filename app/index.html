    How the tracker works

    • Tick a trophy and it is saved in your browser (localStorage) — nothing leaves your machine.
    • Counters (for example 0/42 on the Shooting Range) are saved the same way, with + and − buttons on each counted trophy.
    • Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; Import reads one back.
    • The spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you have earned them.
    • This guide is static files only and works with the network off: it fetches nothing, and the links above open only if you follow them.
